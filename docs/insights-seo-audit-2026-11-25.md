# Wishly Insights SEO audit — 25 November 2026

## Public section and URL structure

- The public editorial area is named **Insights** in the site navigation and footer.
- Insights index: `/insights/`.
- Article URLs: `/insights/{slug}/`.
- The former `/guides/` and `/guides/{slug}/` routes are replaced after export with static zero-second meta-refresh + `location.replace()` pages and canonical targets for GitHub Pages. This is not a server-side HTTP 301. `public/_redirects` retains native 301 rules for hosts such as Cloudflare Pages and Netlify.
- The Insights index is server-rendered and lists every article as a normal link.
- The sitemap, homepage cards, related-article links, country-page related links, footer, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` point to the Insights URL structure.
