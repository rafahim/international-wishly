## How to use this map

These are keyword targets and search-intent clusters based on the language and page structures visible on comparable tools and the questions their landing pages answer. They are **not** verified monthly search volumes, keyword difficulty scores or a guarantee of ranking. Connect Google Search Console and a keyword planner before making volume-based decisions.

Use one main intent per URL. Keep the exact phrase natural in the title, H1 or opening paragraph when it reads well. Use related phrases in useful subheadings and body copy only where they fit. Do not paste the entire list into a page, hidden block, image alt text or every heading.

## Search intent observed in comparable products

A review of public competitor pages on 24 November 2026 found repeated content patterns:

1. A clear product phrase in the hero, such as “birthday website”, “love page” or “online invitation website”.
2. Occasion-led discovery: birthday, anniversary, apology, Valentine, proposal, invitation and friendship categories.
3. A short how-it-works explanation: choose a design, personalise words/photos, publish and share a link.
4. Recipient-specific questions, especially girlfriend, boyfriend, best friend and long-distance partner.
5. Practical reassurance: mobile access, whether an account is needed, sharing method, privacy and what features are actually supported.
6. Helpful supporting content: writing prompts, examples, FAQs, checklists and comparisons—not just a list of keywords.

Wishly should be accurate about its own product limits: editing, account features, premium status, media and interactive controls can vary by template. All account, editor, dashboard, checkout, demo and published-page actions belong on `wishly.bd`.

## Technical and editorial checklist

- Keep one descriptive title and one clear H1 per indexable page.
- Write a unique meta description that explains the page rather than repeating a keyword list.
- Keep canonical URLs on `wishly.rafahim.com` and include every public guide in `sitemap.xml`.
- Use descriptive internal links between related guides, occasion pages and template details.
- Use descriptive image alt text for meaningful images; leave decorative images empty.
- Add visible author information, a genuine about/contact route and real product details.
- Review content for accuracy when template functionality, pricing or product flows change.
- Track impressions and queries in Search Console. Update pages based on real query data, not assumed volume.
- Earn relevant mentions from design, gifting, relationship, student, event and creator communities. Do not buy spam links.
